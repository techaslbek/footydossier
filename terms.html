Footy Dossier Publisher: Terms of Service

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Footy Dossier Publisher ("the App") is a private publishing tool operated by Footy Dossier (@footydossier), a football media brand. The App is used only by Footy Dossier's own editor to prepare and publish Footy Dossier's own content to Footy Dossier's own social media accounts.

  1. Who can use it. The App is not offered to the public. It is used only with accounts owned and controlled by Footy Dossier.
  2. What it does. With the account owner's permission, the App uploads videos, images and captions created by Footy Dossier to that owner's own accounts (e.g. as drafts in the TikTok inbox or as posts). Every post is reviewed and approved by the editor before it is uploaded or published.
  3. Platform rules. Use of the App is subject to the terms and policies of each platform it connects to (including TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms, and Meta's Platform Terms). Footy Dossier publishes only content it has the right to publish.
  4. Revoking access. The account owner can disconnect the App at any time in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).
  5. No warranty. The App is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
